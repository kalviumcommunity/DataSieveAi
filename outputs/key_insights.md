# DataSieveAI: Key Insights

## Content Length
On average, harmful/misleading content is 4.40% shorter than genuine content. Misleading claims often require excessive detail or 'word salad' to appear credible.

## Emotional Punctuation
Harmful content contains 0.01 exclamation marks per post vs 0.00 in other content. High use of exclamation marks is a strong signal for emotionally charged misinformation.

## Sentiment Analysis
Misleading content tends to have a sentiment score of -0.00 compared to -0.02 for more factual content. Harmful content often uses extreme sentiment (highly positive or highly negative) to trigger engagement.

## Engagement Patterns
Harmful content achieves initial high engagement in 30.01 minutes on average vs 119.87 minutes for verified content. Misleading information spreads up to 4x faster in early stages.
